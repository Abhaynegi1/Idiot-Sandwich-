# IDIOT SANDWICH — UI & UX Design Specification

> Visual direction, art style guidelines, screen layouts, animation choreography, and comedic interface tones.

---

## 🎨 Visual Identity & Aesthetic Philosophy

**IDIOT SANDWICH** must look and feel like an intentionally absurd, high-octane internet artifact.

### What It Must NOT Look Like:
- ❌ **Enterprise Software:** No neutral gray dashboards, dense data tables, or corporate minimalism.
- ❌ **SaaS Tool:** No pricing tiers, feature checklists, or productivity widgets.
- ❌ **Generic AI Wrapper:** No chatbot message bubbles, typing dots, or sterile assist prompts.
- ❌ **Crypto / Web3 Site:** No neon hex grids, speculative coins, or faux-cyberpunk gimmicks.
- ❌ **Cluttered Mobile Game:** No popup microtransaction badges, stamina meters, or sensory overload banners.

### What It MUST Look Like:
- ✅ **Bold, Chunky Typography:** Impactful headings, comic book weight, and unmistakable readability.
- ✅ **Mouth-Watering Culinary Surrealism:** Hero-sized visual artwork of impossible sandwiches with studio lighting.
- ✅ **Punchy Micro-Animations:** Screen shakes, bread-slam impacts, sauce splatters, and confetti bursts.
- ✅ **Minimalist, High-Focus Layout:** 90% of the screen is devoted to the clash; zero cognitive overhead for the player.
- ✅ **Deadpan & Sarcastic Copy:** System voice treats ridiculous culinary battles with exaggerated tournament seriousness.

## 📱 Planned Screen Blueprints

```text

│  3. The Clash Animation (The Confrontation)            │
│  4. Victory / Defeat Overlay (The Verdict)             │
│  5. Champion Lineage Drawer (The Historical Chain)     │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

### Screen 1: Landing / Start Screen
- **Hero Title:** Massive stylized logo: **IDIOT SANDWICH** with an animated bread slice framing the title.
- **Hook Headline:** *"Can anything defeat the current champion?"*
- **Current Global / Seed Champion Teaser:** Live card showing the active champion ready for combat.
- **Primary CTA:** Pulsing button: `[ ENTER THE ARENA ]` or immediate focus on the challenge input.

---

### Screen 2: The Arena Screen (Core Gameplay)
- **Top Half — Reigning Champion:**
  - Giant illustrated card of the current champion sandwich.
  - Golden crown badge + Defense Counter (e.g., `"Reign: 3 Contenders Crushed"`).
  - Title & comedic culinary flavor text.
- **Center — The Versus Badge:**
  - High-voltage lightning or bread-knife icon dividing the combatants.
- **Bottom Half — The Challenger Forge (Input):**
  - High-contrast text field: *"Enter anything in the universe..."*
  - Instant submit button: `[ TOAST IT! ]` or `[ CLASH ]`.
  - Rotating prompt suggestions below: *"Try: A black hole, warm milk, Abraham Lincoln, quantum foam..."*

---

### Screen 3: The Clash Animation
- **Choreography:**
  1. The screen darkens; spotlight shines on the Champion and Challenger.
  2. The challenger card slides or slams down opposite the champion.
  3. Visual standoff: Bread cards shake with tension particles.
  4. Impact shockwave when the AI verdict is resolved.

---

### Screen 4: Battle Result & Verdict
- **Winner Presentation:**
  - Giant banner: **NEW CHAMPION CROWNED!** or **CHAMPION REIGNS SUPREME!**
  - **The Roast / Rationale Box:** Prominent speech card with the humorous AI reason.
  - Dynamic score breakdown or funny metric comparison.
- **Continuation CTA:**
  - Auto-focused input to immediately submit the next challenger.

---

### Screen 5: Lineage Chain (Champion History)
- **Visual Presentation:** A horizontal or vertical breadcrumb reel displaying every sandwich that held the crown.
- Clicking any past champion opens its historical dossier, defeat cause, and original artwork.

---

## 🎭 Tone of Voice & Copywriting Rules

- **Earnest Absurdity:** The game treats the clash between an onion and a nuclear warhead with the solemn gravity of an Olympic gold medal match.
- **Food Terminology Everywhere:** Turns are "Bites", scores are "Calories" or "Toastiness", defeats are "Stale" or "Crumbs".
- **Zero Jargon:** No AI buzzwords (never say "According to LLM inference"); speak directly as the Arena Announcer.
