# IDIOT SANDWICH — Conceptual Game Rules

> The 10 fundamental conceptual laws governing entities, matchups, state changes, and boundaries in Idiot Sandwich.

---

## 📜 The Ten Core Rules

These rules define the conceptual foundation of the game. They establish how entities interact and what players can expect, while deliberately leaving mathematical scoring formulas adaptable.

---

### Rule 1 — Anything Can Be Submitted
**The universe of challengers is unbounded.**  
A player may submit any natural-language term, physical object, living organism, abstract thought, fictional archetype, or nonsensical combination. The system must never restrict player creativity to a predefined catalog or dictionary.

---

### Rule 2 — Every Submission Becomes an Entity
**Raw input must be crystallized into a game entity.**  
Text typed by the player does not remain mere freeform strings; it is processed into a structured entity with a canonical title, descriptive domain, and sandwich interpretation before entering gameplay.

---

### Rule 3 — Every Entity Can Challenge the Current Champion
**There are no ineligible matchups.**  
Whether it is an ant confronting a galaxy cluster or existential dread confronting a grilled cheese, any valid entity is eligible to initiate a challenge against the reigning champion.

---

### Rule 4 — Every Outcome Requires Understandable Reasoning
**Outcomes must be justified, never arbitrary.**  
The system must never declare a winner without articulating why. The rationale should be funny, intuitive, and defensible within the context of the two entities (e.g., explaining scale disparities, elemental dominance, conceptual ironies, or absurd mechanics).

---

### Rule 5 — Winning Creates a New Champion
**The throne belongs to the victor.**  
If the challenger prevails in the matchup evaluation, it immediately unseats the reigning champion, becomes the new benchmark entity, and resets the active title defense counter.

---

### Rule 6 — Losing Does Not Replace the Champion
**Failed challenges cement champion supremacy.**  
If the challenger falls short, the reigning champion remains on the throne, increments its successful defense counter, and waits for a more formidable or clever contender.

---

### Rule 7 — Game State Should Be Reproducible Where Practical
**Identical clashes should produce consistent conclusions.**  
While humorous prose can vary slightly in flavor, submitting identical entities under the same conditions should yield consistent winner outcomes. Random flip-flopping undermines player agency and strategic experimentation.

---

### Rule 8 — Absurd Inputs Receive Absurd Respect
**The system respects the specifics of hyperbole.**  
A `"toaster that shoots guided nuclear missiles"` must not be reduced to a generic `"toaster"`. The system must acknowledge, evaluate, and depict the specific nuances, absurdities, and modifiers submitted by the player.

---

### Rule 9 — Unsafe or Malicious Inputs Are Handled Gracefully
**Creativity is infinite; malice and harm are bounded.**  
Inputs containing severe hate speech, explicit illegal acts, or prompt injection attacks must be intercepted gracefully. The engine should neutralize or reject the input with comedic deflection rather than crashing, leaking secrets, or rendering harmful content.

---

### Rule 10 — Rules Must Remain Understandable to the Player
**Complexity belongs behind the curtain.**  
Regardless of how sophisticated the underlying semantic models, vector embeddings, or multi-factor calculations are, the outcome presented to the player must feel straightforward, entertaining, and instantly readable.
